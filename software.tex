\makerubrichead{Research software}
Developed open-source simulation software includes
\begin{itemize}
    \item \href{https://github.com/merzbild/Merzbild.jl}{A variable-weight Direct Simulation Monte Carlo code}
    \item \href{https://github.com/knstmrd/paper_ec_trixi_chem}{Extensions of Trixi.jl to entropy-conservative simulations of reacting multi-species gas flows}
    \item \href{https://github.com/lkampoli/kappa}{A library for calculation of thermodynamic and transport properties of non-equilibrium gas flows}
    \item \href{https://github.com/knstmrd/VT-relaxation}{A tool for calculation of vibrational relaxation times based on a kinetic theory definition}
\end{itemize}
